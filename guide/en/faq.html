FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create a separate account?

No. Just sign in with your Google account and you're ready to go.

How much can I use for free?

Core features like your itinerary, notes, and expense tracking are free to use. More capacity or extra features may be added later as optional add-ons.

Can I plan a trip together with friends?

Inviting friends to plan a trip together is coming soon, and it will be completely free.

Can I track expenses in multiple currencies?

Yes — pick a currency per expense, and totals are calculated automatically per currency.

What happens to my data if I delete my account?

Requested deletions are processed without delay, except where retention is legally required. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I use it offline?

Your trip saves automatically while you're online. If your connection drops while you're using it, what you typed stays in place and saves automatically once you're back online.
