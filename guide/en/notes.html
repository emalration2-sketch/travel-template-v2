Core Feature

Notes & Checklists

Switch between free-form notes and checklists to track packing lists or reminders.

A note switched to checklist mode

Write a note

Tap "+ Add note" in the Notes tab and write a title and content freely.

Switch to a checklist

Tap the icon next to a note's title to turn each line into a checklist item. Tap again to switch back — the done-marker stays in the text.

Completed items

Checked items move automatically under "Completed." Tap to collapse or expand that section.

Checkboxes work in View mode too — handy for packing while you travel.
