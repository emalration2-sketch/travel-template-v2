Core Feature

Tracking Expenses

Attach an expense to any stop, and see totals broken down by currency.

Expense tab with currency totals

Add an expense

Tap the coin icon next to any stop to open the expense form. Enter an item name, amount, currency, and an optional note.

Multiple currencies

Choose from 15 built-in currencies, or type a custom one under "Other" if yours isn't listed.

Totals by currency

The Expenses tab shows an auto-summed card per currency — handy when your trip crosses borders.

Edit or delete

Reopen any expense from the Expenses tab or the stop's coin icon to change or remove it.
