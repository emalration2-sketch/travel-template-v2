Core Feature

Building an Itinerary

Each day is its own card, so you can lay out places and times in order.

Day cards with timed stops

Add or remove a day

Use "+ Add a day" at the bottom of the Itinerary tab to add more days, and the ✕ on each day card to remove one. (At least one day must remain.)

Add a place and time

Set a time (24-hour dropdowns) and a place or to-do for each stop. Use "+ Add stop" to keep adding stops within a day.

Add a note

Each stop can carry a short note. Paste a URL and it becomes a clickable link automatically.

Jump between days

While scrolling, a translucent day-jump rail appears on the left. Tap a dot to jump straight to that day.

Add travelers

Add or remove names in the traveler chips at the top. (This is just a display label, not shared accounts — real-time collaboration is coming soon.)
