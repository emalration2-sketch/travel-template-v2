A Little Something Extra

Winging It Mode

You don't have to fill every hour of the plan — meet two spontaneous moments a day.

The Winging It mode screen

What's the winging-it switch?

There's a small switch at the right end of the tab row in the editor. Turn it on and today's 2 spontaneous missions appear as cards — anything from photo-composition challenges to short breaks and small acts of kindness.

How are today's missions chosen?

Once per day, per account, 2 are picked at random. Reopen it later the same day and you'll see the same 2.

Not feeling it?

Tap "Draw again" as many times as you like for a fresh pair.

This feature carries the app's own philosophy — plan your trip, but don't let the plan box you in.
