[Operator] ("the Operator") complies with the Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws, and processes users' personal information as follows.

---

## 1. Personal Information Items Collected

| Category | Items | Collection method |
|---|---|---|
| Google account authentication (mandatory) | Email address, name (display name), profile photo URL, Google account unique identifier (UID) | Provided by Google when the user logs in with "Continue with Google" |
| Generated in the course of using the Service | Travel titles, itineraries, notes, expense records, reference links; attached images; display settings (theme, avatar) | Entered or uploaded directly by the user |
| Automatically generated information | Data synchronization time (timestamp) | Automatically generated when using the Service |

- The Operator does not collect sensitive information such as resident registration numbers, payment information or location information (GPS). (When paid features are introduced in the future, payment-related information will go through separate notice and consent procedures.)
- Images attached by a user may contain personal information (such as a name on an airline ticket), and these are stored at the user's choice and responsibility.

## 2. Purposes of Collecting and Using Personal Information

- Login and account identification; storing, viewing and synchronizing travel data per account
- Performing functions necessary to provide the Service (PDF generation, link sharing, retaining display settings)
- Operating and maintaining the Service and responding to inquiries
- Fulfilling statutory obligations and responding to disputes

## 3. Retention and Use Period of Personal Information

- Personal information is retained while the user's account is maintained, and is destroyed without delay immediately upon the user's request to delete account data or termination of use of the Service.
- Where retention is required under applicable laws, the information is stored separately for the relevant period.
- Data of accounts that have not been logged in to for a long time (e.g., 1 year or more) may be destroyed or stored separately after prior notice. (Specific criteria to be finalized at launch.)

## 4. Provision of Personal Information to Third Parties

The Operator does not provide users' personal information to outside parties. However, the following cases are exceptions.

- Where the user has consented in advance
- Where an investigative agency, court, etc. requests it through lawful procedures in accordance with the law

| Entrustee | Entrusted work | Items transferred | Country, time and method of transfer | Retention period |

| Google LLC (Google Firebase) | Account authentication (Firebase Authentication), data storage (Cloud Firestore) | All items in Section 1 above | Transmitted from time to time over the network when using the Service. ## 6. Rights of Data Subjects and How to Exercise Them

Users may exercise the following rights at any time.

- Access to personal information: check the stored data in My Page or the editor after logging in to the Service
- Correction: edit directly within the Service
- Deletion / suspension of processing: use the deletion feature within the Service, or request via [contact email: to be provided]
- Upon receipt of a request, the Operator takes action without delay and notifies the user of the result.

## 7. Procedure and Method of Destroying Personal Information

- Personal information for which grounds for destruction have arisen is destroyed without delay.
- Information in the form of electronic files is permanently deleted by a method that makes recovery or reproduction impossible.

## 8. Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information

- Encryption of transmission sections (HTTPS) applied
- Minimization of database access privileges and authentication-based access control (server-side security rules restrict each user to accessing only their own data)
- Management of administrator accounts and infrastructure access privileges

## 9. Cookies and Local Storage

- The Service does not use cookies for user identification.
- The browser's local storage (localStorage) is used to store display settings (the selected color theme, view/edit mode) and a temporary copy of the trip being edited. These do not identify individuals and are stored only in the user's browser. They are removed when site data is deleted in the browser settings.
